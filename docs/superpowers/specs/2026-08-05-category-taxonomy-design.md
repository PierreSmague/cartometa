# Category taxonomy rework

Replace the six inherited meta categories with seven that actually partition the
corpus, and retag all 4171 stored metas.

## Why

The current taxonomy does not classify. Measured over `data/metas/` and
`data/manual/`:

| Category | Metas | Share |
|---|---|---|
| `autre` | 2435 | 58.4 % |
| `signalisation` | 721 | 17.3 % |
| `vegetation` | 443 | 10.6 % |
| `poteaux` | 324 | 7.8 % |
| `bollards` | 125 | 3.0 % |
| `vehicule` | 123 | 2.9 % |

4171 metas in total: 4149 in `data/metas/`, 22 in `data/manual/`.

A filter where the majority bucket is "other" is not a filter. Sampling `autre`
shows it holds at least five distinct families: terrain and hydrography, Google
car and coverage, road numbering and markings, buildings, and language, script
and flags. Three of them have no category of their own today.

The three narrow "infrastructure" categories (`poteaux`, `bollards`,
`signalisation`) split one family across three filters while the largest
families have none.

## Decisions taken

Recorded here because each one changes which metas move where.

1. **Seven categories, not six.** Terrain and hydrography (~325 metas after
   rule ordering) fit none of the six requested categories. Without a home they
   land in `Other`, which stays the dominant bucket and defeats the rework.
   `Landscape` is added in position 3.
2. **All vegetation goes to `Vegetation & Agriculture`**, cultivated or not.
   Forests, bush, grassland and scrub are vegetation, not landscape.
   `Landscape` keeps relief, hydrography and climate only.
3. **Precedence: the observed object wins, except language and script.** A
   Cyrillic road sign is `Culture`, not `Infrastructures`, because the clue is
   the writing system. Everything else classifies by what the player physically
   looks at: a church is `Architecture`, a silo is `Architecture`, a flag on the
   car antenna is `Car meta`. `Culture` therefore holds only the immaterial.
4. **Rules plus a versioned override file**, not rules alone and not a one-shot
   rewrite of the JSON. `data/metas/` is gitignored and regenerated by
   `cartometa-extract`, which assigns the category through `infer_category()`.
   Any judgement stored only in those files is silently lost on the next
   extract.

## The seven categories

Display order is the order below. It is the order of the filter pills on the
site and of the options in the review form.

| # | Site label | Data slug | Scope | Estimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Infrastructures | `infrastructure` | Poles, bollards, signs and their shape, road markings, guardrails, kerbs, bridges, tunnels, bus stops, power lines, road numbering, paving | 1554 |
| 2 | Vegetation & Agriculture | `vegetation` | Every kind of vegetation, cultivated or wild: crops, orchards, plantations, greenhouses, pastures, paddies, vineyards, forests, bush, grassland, scrub | 419 |
| 3 | Landscape | `landscape` | Relief, hydrography, climate: mountains, hills, ridges, valleys, lakes, rivers, coasts, cliffs, deserts as landform, volcanoes, snow, glaciers, canyons | 325 |
| 4 | Architecture | `architecture` | Anything built: houses, roofs, facades, balconies, walls, fences, churches, mosques, temples, silos, barns, monuments, museums | 413 |
| 5 | Car meta | `car` | The Google vehicle and its capture: camera, antenna, blur, rift, snorkel, coverage generations, trekker, season and time of day | 723 |
| 6 | Culture | `culture` | The immaterial only: language, writing system, flags, national colours, religion, customs, internet domains, currency | 230 |
| 7 | Other | `autre` | What none of the six covers: administrative facts, context notes, isolated cases | 507 |

Estimates come from a prototype of the classifier run over the real corpus. They
are what the rules alone produce, before human review, and will shift.

`autre` keeps its slug. It is already the stored value for 2414 metas and for 21
of the 22 versioned manual metas; renaming it would migrate the majority of the
corpus for a cosmetic gain. `vegetation` keeps its slug for the same reason. The
five new slugs are English, like the rest of the project.

## Precedence, stated as rules

Evaluated in this order, first match wins:

0. **Language or writing system mentioned** → `culture`. This is the one
   exception to object-wins, and it is evaluated before everything else, over
   the title and the description **together**. That last part matters:
   `infer_category` resolves the title completely before looking at the
   description, so a title naming the sign would otherwise settle the category
   before the description could mention the script.
1. `car` — the vehicle and the capture itself.
2. `infrastructure`
3. `architecture`
4. `vegetation`
5. `landscape`
6. `culture` — flags, religion, customs, domains, currency.
7. `autre` — no rule matched.

The order after step 0 is load-bearing, and it is where errors will concentrate.
"A road winds through the mountains" resolves to `infrastructure` because the
road matches first; that follows from object-wins but a reader may expect
`landscape`. Cases like this are what the override file is for. Do not reorder
these rules to fix an individual meta — add an override.

## Mechanism

`cartometa/extract/categories.py` becomes the single owner of the taxonomy: the
ordered tuple of the seven slugs, the language exception, and the ordered rules.
`infer_category(title, description)` keeps its signature, so `cartometa-extract`
and the review form's `/api/category` suggestion follow without change.

`data/categories.json` is versioned and holds human corrections, keyed by
country then by meta id:

```json
{ "FR": { "Izqw": "landscape" }, "PH": { "man-0b8e": "architecture" } }
```

**Keyed by country, not by id alone.** Meta ids are not globally unique: `yHR2`
exists in both AT and ES, `Izqw` in CA and FR, `pf4x` in FR and KE. A flat
`{id: slug}` file would apply one correction to two unrelated metas.

It is applied on read in `build_dataset()` — one place, authoritative for the
published site whatever happens to the regenerable files. An id absent from the
file keeps its inferred category. An id present in the file but unknown to the
country being built is reported as a build warning, alongside the existing
orphan and legacy-status warnings: a typo in a hand-edited override must not
pass unnoticed. Scoping the check by country is what makes it usable — a
contributor running `cartometa-build FR` must not be warned about every other
country's overrides.

One-off migration of the stored values, so the reviewer stops showing
`signalisation` and the manual form's valid values match:

- `data/metas/*.json` — regenerable, migrated anyway for coherence.
- `data/manual/*/metas.json` — versioned, 22 metas.

## Retagging protocol

Rules do the bulk. Three sets are then read by hand:

- the metas that land in `Other`, to recover what the rules missed;
- the known collision families — sign plus script (~95), religious buildings
  (~32), farm buildings (~10), car plus flag (~9);
- a random sample of 100 among the classified ones, to estimate the error rate.

Every deviation from the rules goes into `data/categories.json`. Anything
genuinely uncertain is submitted for validation, grouped by pattern rather than
one meta at a time.

## Surfaces touched

- `viewer/index.html` — seven filter pills, in order, new labels and
  `data-categorie` values.
- `cartometa/review/static/index.html` — the `<select>` options.
- `cartometa/review/manual.py` — the `CATEGORIES` tuple that validates entry.
- `cartometa/build/dataset.py` — override application and the unknown-id report.
- `tests/test_categories.py` — rewritten for the new taxonomy, including the
  language exception and the rule order.
- `docs/adding-a-meta-by-hand.md` — the category table and the note explaining
  which values stay French and why.

The site changes only after `cartometa-build` and a redeploy.

## Testing

`test_categories.py` covers: one case per category, the language exception
beating object-wins, the rule order on a deliberately ambiguous input, and the
fallback to `autre`. New tests in `test_build_dataset.py` cover: an override
replacing an inferred category, an absent id keeping its category, and an
unknown id being reported.

The 273 existing tests must stay green. `test_store.py` and
`test_build_dataset.py` use `"autre"` in fixtures, which survives the rework.

## Out of scope

Renaming `autre` to `other`. Renaming the French identifiers in the code.
Reclassifying by anything other than title and description — no image analysis.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `Other` holds under 15 % of stored metas.
2. No category is empty.
3. Every stored meta carries one of the seven slugs; no meta keeps `poteaux`,
   `bollards`, `signalisation` or `vehicule`.
4. `data/categories.json` is versioned and applied by the build; deleting it
   changes categories but never breaks the build. An override for an id the
   built country does not have produces a warning, not silence.
5. The seven pills appear on the site in the specified order and each filters to
   a non-empty gallery on at least one point of the map.
6. Full suite green.
